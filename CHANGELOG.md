## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-12

First release.

- Effect **3D Cube**: a clip on the faces of a box that can be turned, tipped, rolled, moved in
  three dimensions and resized with keyframes; constant spin; cube or frame-shaped box; shading,
  opacity, transparent or coloured background.
- Faces show the clip, up to six video or image files, the cells of a 3 × 2 grid, or a colour.
- Same-track transitions *turn left / turn right / tip up / tip down / flip card* and two
  compositions for use between tracks.
- Helper effect *3D Cube: grid cell* to feed timeline clips to the faces through a sequence.
- `cube3d-faces`: makes the clips and zones of a project bin pickable as face files.
- Prebuilt for Linux x86-64 (needs glibc 2.34 or newer; loads in the Kdenlive 26.08 Flatpak).
